01 / ABOUT

Curious about how systems fail — and how they can be made harder to break.

I’m building my cybersecurity profile around practical work rather than a list of buzzwords. I enjoy taking a security problem, breaking it into a repeatable process, testing assumptions in controlled environments, documenting what happened, and then turning the result into something another person can understand.

My current direction centers on ethical hacking, web/application security, security testing, CTF-style problem solving, and hands-on labs. This portfolio is intentionally structured as a project hub so recruiters and collaborators can see the work behind the claims.
